# PV013 — Finance RAG grouped selection with primary-driver classification

Use only the supplied grouped material-consequence sentence catalog derived from a Form 10-K Item 1A packet. Identify exactly three distinct material risks. The deterministic lexical screen is only a search aid, not a gold answer.

For each risk, select one sentence that states both a concrete risk driver and an explicit consequence for the filer. Prefer broad or severe consequences affecting operations, financial condition, results, customers, or reputation. Do not select a heading, fragment, generic background statement, or a sentence whose consequence belongs only to an uncited prior sentence.

Each record has the form `[E0001 | GROUP G001] complete evidence sentence`. Select exactly one record from each of three different GROUP values. Return only its `evidence_id`; the deterministic locator restores the exact quote, paragraph, and page. Do not quote or rewrite source text.

Return one JSON object only. Copy the harness `case_id`, `model_id`, and `prompt_version`. `risks` must contain exactly three objects ordered `R1`, `R2`, `R3`, with only these fields: `risk_id`, `risk_summary`, `risk_type`, and `evidence_id`.

`risk_summary` must be a concrete 4–12 word noun phrase naming the source-supported risk driver. It must not equal the category label and must not add a product, activity, cause, or consequence absent from the selected sentence.

Classify the primary risk driver, not merely the downstream consequence:

- `Strategic / Market / Technology`: competition, demand, asset or product prices, adoption, pricing, strategic partnerships, or technological change.
- `Operational / Supply Chain`: operational service dependency or disruption, production, delivery, suppliers, infrastructure, or third-party service performance. A strategic partnership is not operational merely because a third party is involved.
- `Regulatory / Legal / Geopolitical`: laws, regulations, enforcement, litigation, fines, sanctions, or political/trade restrictions.
- `Financial / Liquidity / Credit`: liquidity, credit, lending, funding, insurance sufficiency, counterparty default, or fee/funding structure. Do not use this category merely because a market or operational risk affects revenue or financial condition.
- `Cybersecurity / Data / Privacy`: security, data, privacy, unauthorized access, or a breach.
- `Other`: a central theme such as intellectual-property protection that does not fit the five categories above.

The three evidence IDs must exist, be different, come from three different GROUP values, and represent three different risk themes. Never add facts from memory.
