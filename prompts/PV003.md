Emit only the final JSON object; do not emit analysis, Markdown, or a schema explanation. Copy the harness values for `case_id`, `model_id`, and `prompt_version`. For every risk, copy a short evidence substring exactly as printed, including punctuation. Prefer text contained on one physical input line. Cite the exact paragraph marker that contains the quote and the integer from its nearest preceding `PDF_PAGE` marker. Never cite a heading paragraph for text that appears in the following paragraph. Check those three conditions before final output. Do not invent controls, mitigation, dollar effects, probabilities, or forecasts.
